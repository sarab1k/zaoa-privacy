Privacy Policy - ZAOA

Last Updated: November 2026

Developed by: NOAH

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the ZAOA mobile application. The application is designed, built, and maintained by NOAH as an independent developer. ZAOA is an e-commerce showcase application for accessories including watches, eyewear, and related goods.

2. Application Permissions

Zero Permissions Required: ZAOA does not request, require, or access any sensitive Android system permissions.

  • No Storage access (READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).
  • No Camera access.
  • No Location services (GPS or Network location).
  • No access to contacts, microphone, or system settings.

3. Data Collection and Usage

We respect your privacy completely. The application operates as a read-only showcase catalog. We do not collect, store, transmit, process, or sell any personal data (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, device identifiers, or financial information).

4. Third-Party Services & Links

When users wish to inquire about or purchase an item displayed in ZAOA, the app redirects them via external links to official support channels (such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, or Facebook). The app itself does not process payments or collect billing info. Third-party platforms operate under their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

The content within ZAOA consists solely of general commercial fashion accessories and is safe for general audiences. The application does not target children under the age of 13 nor does it knowingly collect any data from them.

6. Security

Because our application does not collect or store any user data, there is no risk of user data exposure or compromise through the application.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates become effective immediately upon being published on this page.
