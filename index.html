Privacy Policy for ZAOA

Last Updated: November 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ZAOA ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information and clarifies that we do not collect, store, or process any personal data through the ZAOA mobile application.

2. Developer & App Information

  • Application Name: ZAOA
  • Developer: NOAH DEV (Individual Developer)
  • App Category: E-Commerce / Accessories Store
  • Contact & Ordering: Product inquiries and purchases are facilitated externally via Telegram channels.

3. Data Collection and Usage

ZAOA does not collect, retain, or process any personal user data.

  • No Registration Required: Users can browse products freely without creating an account or submitting personal credentials (such as name, email address, or phone number).
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use third-party tracking software, SDKs, or analytics services to monitor or record user behavior.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: The application does not serve ads or integrate advertising frameworks.

4. App Permissions

The ZAOA app is designed with a strong focus on security and privacy. The application does not request or require any sensitive runtime permissions, including but not limited to:

  • Device Storage / Media Access
  • Camera or Microphone
  • Precise or Approximate GPS Location
  • Contacts or Phone State

5. Third-Party Links & Communication (Telegram)

Our application contains direct links and interactive buttons that open external conversations via Telegram to process customer inquiries and orders. Once you leave the ZAOA app to interact on Telegram, any information you provide is governed by Telegram's official Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We do not store or transmit any conversation logs or user details inside the ZAOA app itself.

6. Children's Privacy and Target Audience

ZAOA is a general audience application suitable for users of all ages, including children, as it strictly displays accessory product listings (e.g., watches, eyewear). It does not contain inappropriate content, age-restricted material, or data collection mechanisms targeting minors.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out to us via our official Telegram support channel.
